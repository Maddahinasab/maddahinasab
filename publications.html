Research, White Papers & Legal Analysis

My work spans energy law, investment law, contractual governance, foreign investment, and transitional legal risk. The materials below include peer-reviewed academic scholarship as well as applied legal analysis and white papers addressing institutional volatility, regulatory governance, and market-entry risk in evolving jurisdictions.

Featured White Papers & Advisory Publications

Iran Transitional Legal Risk Framework™

A white paper examining legal and institutional risks associated with foreign market entry into Iran under conditions of political transition, regulatory uncertainty, and sanctions-related complexity.

The framework provides structured legal risk assessment for foreign investors, SMEs, advisory firms, and institutions seeking to evaluate transitional legal exposure and market-entry feasibility.
