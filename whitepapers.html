Afghanistan Transitional Risk Outlook

Legal Survivability Under De Facto Authority and Post-Conflict Institutional Gaps

A structured risk outlook evaluating investment durability in Afghanistan under conditions of de facto governance, post-conflict institutional fragmentation, and suspended international legal recognition. Examines contractual survivability where the formal legal system has fragmented and authority has shifted to actors operating outside recognised governance frameworks.

Addresses the specific challenge of maintaining legal position in environments where enforcement depends not on formal legal order but on the practical authority of de facto power structures — and how investors can structure against that reality.
