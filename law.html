Advisory framework

Iran Transitional Legal Risk Framework™

A structured methodology for identifying and assessing legal, contractual, and institutional risks in transitional jurisdictions.

The framework addresses regulatory instability, enforcement uncertainty, sanctions layering, contractual survivability, and institutional continuity risk.

It is designed for investors, institutions, and advisors working in legally sensitive and high-volatility environments.
