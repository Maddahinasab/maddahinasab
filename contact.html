What we engage on

We work with foreign investors, institutional actors, fund managers, and researchers operating in or considering entry to transitional, frontier, and institutionally volatile markets.

  • Advisory mandates Transitional risk assessments, market entry structuring, sanctions intelligence, contractual resilience reviews.
  • MTRIS™ assessments Commissioned application of the MTRIS™ framework to a specific market, transaction, or investment scenario.
  • Research collaboration Academic partnerships, joint publications, and institutional research initiatives in investment law and transitional governance.
  • Expert network inquiries Practitioners, academics, and regional specialists interested in joining the advisory network.
  • Speaking & conferences Keynotes, panel contributions, and expert commentary on transitional market law and investment risk.

All engagements are handled with strict confidentiality. We do not share client information or inquiry details with any third party.
