About the Publication

Legal analysis built for the pace of transitional markets

Each issue follows the same disciplined sequence: establishing the relevant developments and the background needed to understand them, assessing the legal mechanics at play, drawing out the commercial and investment implications, and closing with a forward-looking scenario and MTRIS™ decision framework.

The first three monthly issues are devoted to a single case — the renewed escalation between the United States and Iran — each applying the same underlying analysis through a different sector: energy, then supply chains and transportation, then banking and financial services. A quarterly report consolidates all three into one holistic assessment.

Written by Mostapha Maddahinasab, Ph.D. Founder, Maddahinasab Legal Advisory · Architect of MTRIS™
