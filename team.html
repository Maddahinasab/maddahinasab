Leadership and collaboration

A Structured Advisory Network

Maddahinasab Legal Advisory brings together proprietary methodology and specialist collaboration to support legal due diligence, regulatory analysis, investment strategy, and institutional risk assessment in transitional markets.

The network is designed to remain flexible and project-specific, allowing the firm to engage relevant expertise based on jurisdiction, sector, transaction profile, and the complexity of the legal or regulatory environment.

  • Transitional legal and regulatory risk assessment
  • Institutional and governance analysis
  • Energy law, oil & gas, and renewable energy regulation
  • Public policy and regulatory governance
  • Commercial risk and legal due diligence
  • Sanctions-related legal and market considerations
  • Regional political and economic analysis

Founder and Principal Advisor

Mostapha Maddahinasab, Ph.D.

Founder and Principal Advisor

Dr. Mostapha Maddahinasab is a legal scholar and advisory practitioner specializing in investment law, energy law, transitional legal risk, and regulatory governance in evolving and high-risk markets. He earned his Ph.D. in Oil and Gas Law from the University of Tehran and conducted research at the University of Zürich, followed by postdoctoral research at Ruhr University Bochum. His recent work focuses on legal risk, institutional volatility, sanctions-related exposure, and foreign investment in transitional environments. The firm’s flagship methodology, MTRIS™, reflects this work by offering a disciplined framework for assessing legal-strategic exposure in transitional markets.
