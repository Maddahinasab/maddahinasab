Flagship Methodology

The MTRIS™
Framework

The Maddahinasab Transitional Risk Intelligence System™ is a proprietary legal-strategic framework designed to evaluate investment resilience in environments where formal law and institutional reality diverge. Applied in three analytical layers, evaluated holistically.

Institutional Layer

Assessment of governance legitimacy, political authority structures, and regulatory continuity. Identifies whether the institutions that back your legal position are themselves stable, legitimate, and capable of enforcement.

Can the state uphold your legal position if political conditions shift?

Transactional Layer

Evaluation of contractual robustness, title integrity, and legal survivability under conditions of regime change, sanctions exposure, or institutional collapse. Goes beyond formal validity to test structural durability.

Will your deal hold under a governance shock or regulatory reversal?

Operational Layer

Analysis of practical feasibility, compliance operability, and execution risk in environments with fragmented governance or informal authority. Maps the gap between legal rights and operational reality on the ground.

Can you operate without the institutional support your contract assumes?

Common Questions

What is MTRIS™?

MTRIS™ (the Maddahinasab Transitional Risk Intelligence System) is a proprietary legal-strategic framework created by Mostapha Maddahinasab, Ph.D. It evaluates investment resilience across three layers — Institutional (governance legitimacy), Transactional (contractual survivability), and Operational (execution feasibility) — to assess whether a legal position will hold if political or regulatory conditions shift.

What is a "transitional market"?

A transitional market is a jurisdiction where institutional continuity, regulatory predictability, and legal durability cannot be assumed — typically due to political volatility, fragmented governance, sanctions exposure, or post-conflict reconstruction. This differs from "frontier" or "emerging" market classifications, which focus primarily on economic development stage rather than institutional stability.

Who does Maddahinasab Legal Advisory work with?

The firm advises foreign investors, private equity and fund managers, law firms requiring specialist co-counsel, institutional actors, and energy, mining, and infrastructure companies operating in or considering entry to transitional and sanctions-sensitive markets. Mandates are accepted on a selective basis.
